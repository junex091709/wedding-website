# Wedding invitation music

Add the downloaded, licensed Canon in D piano MP3 to this folder with the exact filename:

`canon-in-d-piano.mp3`

The website expects it at `assets/canon-in-d-piano.mp3`.

Suggested recording: Ava, “Canon in D Major (piano only)” on Free Music Archive, licensed CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://freemusicarchive.org/music/ava-drumm/single/canon-in-d-major-piano-only/

Download the MP3 from the track page and upload it here. The envelope animation and music controls are already wired to this path. Until the MP3 is added, the music will not play.
